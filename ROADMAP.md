# Product roadmap

Roadmap items describe future work; they are not available features. Implementation and release dates are not committed.

## Generative Instagram and Facebook video shorts

Instagram image-to-Reel composition is available in Compose: ordered image scenes, on-screen text, timing, cut/fade transitions, optional uploaded audio, durable rendering, and a reviewed 9:16 video asset. The items below concern future generative video and Facebook-specific workflows.

Create short vertical videos/Reels from campaign context, supplied introduction posts, and uploaded brand assets. A writing model can produce the script and storyboard; rendering requires a separate video-generation or video-composition provider with its own capabilities, credentials, pricing, and limits.

Planned workflow:

1. Choose an account, campaign goal, source posts, brand assets, target duration, and video style.
2. Review an editable script and scene-by-scene storyboard generated by the text model.
3. Choose a video provider/model separately from the text and image providers. Show the estimated cost and duration before starting generation.
4. Generate a vertical 9:16 video, with optional voiceover, captions, branded end card, and appropriately licensed audio.
5. Preview the video, edit or regenerate individual scenes, and save the final asset in the workspace Media Library.
6. Publish or schedule through the Instagram/Facebook video adapters after checking each destination’s current format, duration, permission, and media-access requirements.

Delivery requirements include durable asynchronous jobs, provider progress/cancellation, protected credentials, workspace isolation, cost controls, and retained intermediate assets so a failed render can resume. Generation is reviewed before publishing; a render does not automatically publish to social accounts.

Provider selection and evaluation are future work. Compare output quality, brand fidelity, latency, commercial-use terms, and cost before selecting a default or adding a supported provider. A deterministic slideshow/text-overlay renderer may complement generative video for introductions and feature walkthroughs.
